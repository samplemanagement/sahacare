Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 1, 2026

Information we collect

During waitlist signup, we collect your email address, selected role, and limited technical data such as IP address, browser user agent, and referral source.

How we use information

We use this information to manage the waitlist, send signup confirmations, contact interested users, and improve reliability and security of the website.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with infrastructure providers that help operate the service, including hosting, email delivery, and database platforms.

Data retention

We keep waitlist and operational logs only as long as needed for product research, security, and business operations, unless a longer period is required by law.

Your choices

You may request correction or deletion of your waitlist information by contacting us at hello@sahacare.me.

Security

We use reasonable technical safeguards, but no internet transmission or storage method is fully secure.

Changes

We may update this policy periodically. Material updates will be reflected by changing the effective date.
