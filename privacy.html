Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 1, 2026

Who this policy applies to

This Privacy Policy applies to visitors, waitlist signups, and interview or pilot participants using the SahaCare website and related communications.

Information we collect

We may collect contact details (such as email), role selection, optional interview responses, and limited technical information like IP address, user agent, referral source, and timestamped activity logs.

How we use information

We use information to operate the site, manage waitlist and interviews, send confirmations and product updates, improve reliability, prevent abuse, and understand aggregate usage trends.

How we share information

We do not sell personal information. We share data only with service providers that support operations, such as hosting, email delivery, analytics, and secure database infrastructure.

Cookies and analytics

We may use basic cookies or similar technologies for session handling, security controls, and aggregate traffic measurement. You can manage cookies through your browser settings.

Data retention

We retain information only as long as needed for product research, operational security, legal obligations, and legitimate business purposes.

International processing

Depending on infrastructure providers, data may be processed in countries outside your location. We use reasonable safeguards for such transfers.

Your choices and rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your waitlist information by emailing us. We respond within a reasonable time.

Children's privacy

SahaCare is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through this website.

Security

We use administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards, including encryption in transit and at rest where applicable. No method of storage or transmission is fully guaranteed secure.

Changes

We may update this policy periodically. Material updates will be reflected by changing the effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions, email us.
